# Korean Filing Evidence

For Korean company financial statements and statutory disclosure details, find and inspect the original report on the Financial Supervisory Service's DART first. Identify the issuer, report, receipt number, date, reporting period, consolidated or separate basis, and relevant statement or note.

Do not use blogs as sources for reported financial figures or disclosure details, even if they claim to reproduce DART. When the original DART report cannot be accessed or the requested detail cannot be verified, say so explicitly and do not present the figure as verified. An original filing published on the Korea Exchange's KIND may be used as an original official document, with its location and any limitation disclosed; do not imply it was accessed on DART when it was not.

Clearly distinguish original disclosures, official company materials, securities-firm research, and other reputable data providers. For forecasts and interpretations, identify their source and distinguish them from reported actuals. This preference does not authorize accessing credentials, broker accounts, or making trades.
