Docs/Improve/tcx-persona

Your investment approach

tcx-persona

Start with an investor-inspired perspective, make it your own, and improve it through trade reviews. Your existing specialist team stays in place.

Choose and personalize #

Ask to compare the 13 included perspectives, then choose one starting lens. Explain your horizon, research method, avoid conditions, and examples. Unknown details remain questions, not invented rules.

$tcx-persona
Show me the available personas. Help me choose a starting lens for my trading method.
$tcx-persona
Start from Warren Buffett. Create investment-brain-my-quality, version 1.0.0, publisher "Workspace owner", license MIT. I focus on understandable businesses and durable cash flow over three to five years. I abstain when debt or cyclicality cannot be explained. Keep my exact entry, exit, and sizing rules in a separate Strategy draft.

You receive a personal source, a summary of adaptations and missing choices, and validation results. The names describe inspired perspectives; they are not endorsements or a promise of returns.

Install, activate, and use #

After reviewing the source, start a new turn for installation. Activation is a separate explicit request for the validated version. See Brain lifecycle.

$tcx-brain
Validate and install investment-brains/investment-brain-my-quality as inactive.
$tcx-brain
Activate investment-brain-my-quality version 1.0.0.
$investment-brain-my-quality
Analyze this company using my lens and show contrary evidence.

The exact active Brain id selects the lens for that run. A persona name or $tcx-persona alone does not select it. Each analysis keeps its version, so later changes do not rewrite the past.

Learn through review #

$tcx-persona
Review investment-brain-my-quality using the decisions and trade reviews I provide below. Separate decision quality from profit or loss. Identify strengths to preserve, weaknesses to address, contrary cases, and one testable improvement. Do not apply a new version yet.

The review proposes changes with supporting and contrary evidence, scope, uncertainty, and a test against the current version. One winning trade does not prove an edge; a loss does not automatically invalidate a sound process.

When ready, ask to apply a specific proposal to your personal source. The revised source gets a higher version and validation. Managed update and activation follow tcx-brain; you can roll back to an installed prior version. Exact trading rules follow Strategy.

Learning changes explicit instructions and recorded lessons. It does not retrain the model, automatically raise risk, or make every review a validated lesson. Missing records and evidence limits remain visible.
