# Review-driven persona improvement

Use this procedure only for the personal Brain and cases selected by the user.
Respect the source-authoring privacy and version rules in tcx-brain and the
recording/adoption/terminal boundaries in tcx-memory. Do not build another
memory database or rewrite historical decisions.

## 1. Establish the comparison

Identify the source id, installed version/digests used for each decision, the
selected Strategy snapshot (or no_strategy), timeframe, regime, and selected
case IDs. Different versions or strategies are different cohorts. Do not
attribute every result to the persona. Ask for an unambiguous target or cases
if missing; do not sweep all private Memory.

A user-supplied anecdote may start a candidate, but label missing decision-time
evidence, selection bias, and unresolved outcomes. Do not fabricate record
IDs. User statements are not authenticated outcome records.

## 2. Separate process from outcome

First reconstruct the decision-time evidence, alternatives, forecasts,
invalidation criteria, rule adherence, and unknowns. Use only information
available at that time. Assess process before inspecting outcome. If P&L is
already known, explicitly label the review outcome-aware; do not claim a blind
process review or backdate a lock.

Then compare the result net of fees, spread, slippage, and relevant financing
costs where available. Include losing trades, successful trades, missed trades,
and no-trade decisions within the selected scope. Do not infer quality from a
winning outcome or a flaw from a single loss.

## 3. Preserve strengths and diagnose weaknesses

For each observation distinguish:
- evidence/data quality;
- a defect or applicability limit in a principle;
- failure to follow an otherwise suitable rule;
- implementation/costs/timing;
- ordinary uncertainty or unresolved attribution.

A **strength** needs a repeatable behavior, supporting cases, contrary cases,
and conditions in which it helps. Reinforce it through a clearer checklist,
better evidence, or stricter application within its valid scope. More leverage,
larger size, and more trades are not automatic ways to amplify a strength.

A **weakness** needs a causal hypothesis, competing explanation, and bounded
replacement or abstention condition. Preserve useful existing principles;
avoid wholesale personality changes after one outcome. Prefer the smallest
change that can be tested independently.

## 4. Return a reviewable proposal

Use a compact table with one row per proposed change:

| Target/version | Preserve or change | Before → proposed | Supporting and contrary case refs | Scope and uncertainty | Test and failure condition | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Add the process/outcome separation, net-cost and baseline assumptions, missing
evidence, and whether a change belongs in Brain, Strategy, Investor Context,
or data collection. Label each lesson candidate, corroborated, validated, or
retired according to actual canonical records. No evidence means candidate,
not validated. Separate historical_replay, historical_holdout, and live_forward.

If persistence is requested, use the existing Memory/Improve owners. A normal
workspace Markdown proposal may be saved outside the Brain as a noncanonical
draft with links to existing records, but it cannot replace a Judgment,
Postmortem, or Lesson record. Never claim a terminal handoff was executed.

## 5. Test before adopting

Predeclare a baseline (current version), the single change, eligible universe
and regimes, evaluation window, transaction-cost model, exposure/risk limits,
process metrics, performance metrics, and rejection criteria. Examples include
rule adherence, unsupported conclusions, net expectancy, drawdown, turnover,
and analysis time/cost. Do not optimize every metric after seeing results.

Use unseen periods or forward paper decisions with enough independent cases
for the claimed scope. Overlapping trades are not independent replications.
Record every attempted variation; a backtest selected after many trials is
not fresh holdout evidence. Keep results for failed candidates. If evidence is
insufficient, keep the change experimental and state what evidence is needed.

Independent contrary-case review and actual out-of-sample evidence are needed
for validated lessons. Native child feedback alone cannot pass the existing
authenticated reviewer gate. Report that limit without manufacturing a role
identity or silently promoting a lesson.

## 6. Revise without erasing history

A request to revise a specifically identified user-owned source authorizes the
reversible source edit once sufficient evidence/privacy choices are supplied.
Abstract lessons into general principles; leave tickers, account facts, case
text, and private record IDs outside the portable bundle.

Follow tcx-brain: choose a version above all installed versions, validate,
show the diff and limitations, and stop before managed update/activation.
Exact trading rules go through tcx-strategy instead of being smuggled into a
Brain. Report the previous baseline and a rollback target after a managed
version change. Compare subsequent reviews against the version actually used.
